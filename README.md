# E-Commerce Customer, Operations & Business Performance Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end Power BI business analytics project designed to analyze
customer behavior, sales performance, product profitability, returns,
and delivery operations for a fictional e-commerce company.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into actionable
business insights that support management decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance
- Analyze customer behavior and retention
- Evaluate product and category profitability
- Identify delivery performance issues
- Analyze customer acquisition channels
- Monitor returns and cancellations
- Identify areas for operational improvement

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization

## 📂 Dataset

The project contains:

- 12,000+ Orders
- 2,000 Customers
- 200 Products
- 12,000 Delivery Records
- Returns Data
- Marketing Data
- Warehouse Data
- Date Dimension

## 🔄 Data Preparation

Power Query was used for:

- Data type correction
- Text standardization
- Trimming inconsistent values
- Cleaning payment methods
- Standardizing order status
- Validating duplicate records
- Preparing tables for relational modeling

## 🏗️ Data Model

The project uses a relational/star-schema-oriented model consisting of:

- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Date
- Warehouses
- Delivery
- Returns
- Marketing

## 📌 Key Insights

- On-time delivery rate was approximately 50.48%.
- Late deliveries represented approximately 43.42% of delivery records.
- Repeat customer rate was approximately 98.60%.
- Maharashtra generated the highest total revenue.
- Organic acquisition generated the highest revenue among acquisition channels.
- Beauty showed stronger category-level profitability compared with lower-margin categories such as Electronics.
- High revenue products were not always the most profitable products.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Investigate major causes of delivery delays.
- Optimize warehouse allocation and dispatch processes.
- Focus on high-margin categories.
- Review pricing and discount strategies for lower-margin categories.
- Strengthen high-performing organic acquisition strategies.
- Analyze return reasons and high-return products.
- Evaluate products using both revenue and profitability metrics.
